# HarborLight Workshop Profitability

HarborLight, a fictional professional-learning company, runs 100 public workshops per year. Profit has declined despite stable enrollment. Leadership wants to identify the cause and restore at least $300,000 of annual profit without reducing teaching quality.

Current economics per workshop: 40 attendees, $500 price per attendee, $8,000 instructor/venue cost, and $120 materials/support cost per attendee. Annual central fixed costs are $900,000.

Two years ago, attendance and price were the same, instructor/venue cost was $7,000, variable cost was $100 per attendee, and central fixed costs were $800,000.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework.
