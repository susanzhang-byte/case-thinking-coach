# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. Revenue: price, attendance, mix, and number of workshops.
2. Variable costs: materials/support per attendee and underlying drivers.
3. Workshop fixed costs: instructor/venue cost and utilization.
4. Central fixed costs and allocation.
5. Actions, feasibility, quality safeguards, and implementation.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

- `Annual revenue = Workshops × Attendees × Price`
- `Annual cost = Workshops × (Workshop fixed cost + Attendees × Variable cost) + Central fixed cost`
- `Profit change = Revenue change − Cost change`

## Worked analysis

Current annual revenue is `100 × 40 × $500 = $2,000,000`. Current cost is `100 × ($8,000 + 40 × $120) + $900,000 = $2,180,000`, producing a $180,000 loss.

Prior cost was `100 × ($7,000 + 40 × $100) + $800,000 = $1,900,000`, producing $100,000 profit. The $280,000 decline comprises $100,000 higher instructor/venue cost, $80,000 higher variable cost, and $100,000 higher central fixed cost.

## Recommendation

Launch a cost program across all three drivers rather than blaming enrollment. Recovering the prior $280,000 still misses the $300,000 goal by $20,000, so pair cost resets with a small, tested revenue or utilization improvement. Protect teaching quality through participant feedback and outcome metrics.
