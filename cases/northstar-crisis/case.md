# Northstar Rewards Messaging Response

Northstar Market, a fictional retailer, announced changes to its rewards program using wording customers interpreted as removing benefits from loyal shoppers. The policy actually changes how points are displayed; average reward value is unchanged. Complaints are increasing on customer-service channels, and several lifestyle writers have repeated the misunderstanding.

The communications lead must recommend a response for the next 72 hours and a recovery plan for the next month.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework.

## Interviewer facts—provide only when requested

- The original announcement was technically accurate but used unfamiliar terminology.
- The company can update its website and contact members directly today.
- Customer-service agents do not yet have a consistent explanation.
- There are no product-safety, legal, political, or personnel issues.
