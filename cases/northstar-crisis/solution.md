# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. Verify the misunderstanding, affected stakeholders, and evidence.
2. Contain confusion: correct owned channels and align customer-facing teams.
3. Communicate: plain-language message, proof/example, messenger, channels, cadence.
4. Engage: direct member clarification and factual outreach to writers.
5. Recover and learn: monitor understanding, revise testing, and establish approval controls.

An alternative timeline structure—today, 24–72 hours, and next month—is equally strong if it covers stakeholders, message, action, and measurement.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

No essential calculation is required. Track message comprehension, contact volume, correction pickup, member engagement, and trust indicators against the pre-announcement baseline.

## Recommendation

Within hours, acknowledge that the explanation caused confusion, state plainly that average reward value is unchanged, and show a before/after example. Update owned channels and give agents one approved explanation. Contact members and writers with the clarification rather than arguing about technical accuracy. Over the month, measure comprehension and trust, and require customer-language testing for future policy communications.
