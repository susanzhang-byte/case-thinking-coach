# BrightBasket Campaign Allocation

BrightBasket is a fictional online home-goods retailer deciding whether to renew a $300,000 creator campaign. A matched-market test estimates 6,000 incremental first orders. Average first-order revenue is $80, gross margin is 45%, and fulfillment cost outside cost of goods is $6 per order.

Leadership asks whether the campaign created positive first-order marketing ROI and how to improve the next allocation.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework.

## Interviewer facts—provide only when requested

- The matched markets were selected before launch and had similar prior trends.
- Creator group A used 60% of spend and generated 70% of incremental orders.
- Creator group B used 40% of spend and generated 30% of incremental orders.
- Customer lifetime value is not yet reliable.
