# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. Objective, baseline, and validity of incrementality evidence.
2. Audience, creative, and funnel performance.
3. Incremental unit economics and ROI.
4. Allocation by creator segment.
5. Test design, longer-term value, and risks.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

- `Incremental contribution per order = Revenue × Gross margin % − Fulfillment cost`
- `Marketing ROI = (Incremental contribution − Campaign cost) ÷ Campaign cost`
- `Cost per incremental order = Spend ÷ Incremental orders`

## Worked analysis

Contribution per order is `$80 × 45% − $6 = $30`. Incremental contribution is `6,000 × $30 = $180,000`. First-order ROI is `($180,000 − $300,000) ÷ $300,000 = −40%`.

Group A spent $180,000 for 4,200 orders, or $42.86 per incremental order. Group B spent $120,000 for 1,800 orders, or $66.67. Both exceed $30 first-order contribution, though A is closer.

## Recommendation

Do not renew the campaign unchanged: first-order ROI is −40%. Run a smaller test concentrated on group A while measuring repeat contribution and testing creative improvements. The central risk is rejecting an acquisition channel before reliable lifetime value is known.
