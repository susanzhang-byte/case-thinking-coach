# LumenDesk Subscription Pricing

LumenDesk, a fictional scheduling tool for small professional-services firms, charges $20 per account per month. It is considering increasing the price to $24. Leadership wants to know whether to raise the price for all customers next quarter.

Current monthly volume is 50,000 accounts. Variable service cost is $5 per account per month. Research suggests a 15% volume decline at $24. Fixed costs will not change.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework.

## Interviewer facts—provide only when requested

- The goal is to improve contribution profit without materially weakening long-term customer trust.
- Competitors charge $18–$27.
- Existing customers represent 80% of accounts and show more resistance than new buyers.
- A grandfathering option would keep existing customers at $20 for six months while pricing new customers at $24; new customers represent 10,000 accounts during that period.
