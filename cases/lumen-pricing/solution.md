# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. Pricing objective and constraints.
2. Customer value, willingness to pay, and segment response.
3. Competitive alternatives and positioning.
4. Economics under price/volume scenarios.
5. Rollout, communication, testing, and retention risk.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

- `Monthly contribution = (Price − Variable cost) × Accounts`
- `Incremental contribution = New contribution − Baseline contribution`
- `Implied elasticity = % volume change ÷ % price change`

## Worked analysis

Baseline contribution is `($20 − $5) × 50,000 = $750,000` monthly. Immediate increase volume is `50,000 × 85% = 42,500`; contribution becomes `($24 − $5) × 42,500 = $807,500`, a $57,500 monthly gain. Price rises 20% and volume falls 15%, implying elasticity of `−0.75` in the research scenario.

## Recommendation

Do not automatically raise every account at once. Pilot $24 with new customers and a clearly communicated, time-limited grandfathering period for existing customers. The full-price scenario improves monthly contribution by $57,500, but segment-level retention evidence is incomplete. Track churn, win rate, support contacts, and contribution by cohort before full rollout.
