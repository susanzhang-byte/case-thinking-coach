# Cedar Office Delivery Turnaround

Cedar Office, a fictional workplace-supplies distributor, promises next-day delivery to small businesses. On-time delivery fell from 96% to 84% over six months while order volume rose 10%. Leadership wants to restore at least 95% service within 90 days without adding permanent distribution sites.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework.

## Interviewer facts—provide only when requested

- Warehouse picking capacity is 12,000 order lines daily; demand now averages 11,400 but reaches 13,500 on Mondays.
- Picking errors rose from 1% to 4% after product locations were reorganized.
- Carrier on-time performance remains 97% for packages handed over by 5 p.m.
- Only 78% of Monday packages are ready by 5 p.m.; other weekdays average 94%.
- Half of late Monday order lines can be shifted to Friday through customer incentives and replenishment reminders.
