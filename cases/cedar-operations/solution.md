# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. Locate the service failure across order intake, picking, packing, handoff, and carrier delivery.
2. Separate demand peaks, effective capacity, productivity, errors/rework, and scheduling.
3. Quantify the Monday gap and test demand-shaping/process fixes.
4. Sequence a 90-day turnaround with owners and leading indicators.
5. Protect customer experience, economics, and operational resilience.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

- `Capacity gap = Peak demand − Available capacity`
- `Shiftable volume = Late/peak volume × Shiftable share`
- `Utilization = Demand ÷ Capacity`
- Track first-pass accuracy and pre-cutoff handoff rate as leading indicators.

## Worked analysis

Monday demand exceeds stated capacity by `13,500 − 12,000 = 1,500` order lines. If half of that excess shifts to Friday, Monday demand falls to 12,750—still 750 above capacity. The location change also tripled the error rate, creating rework. Carrier performance is healthy, so the priority is warehouse flow before handoff.

## Recommendation

Run a 90-day internal-operations turnaround: immediately restore clear location controls and daily error tracking; shift half the Monday excess through reminders/incentives; then recover at least 750 lines of effective Monday capacity through slotting, staffing schedules, and rework reduction. Monitor Monday demand, first-pass accuracy, 5 p.m. handoff, and final on-time delivery. The main risk is treating nominal capacity as usable capacity while errors consume throughput.
