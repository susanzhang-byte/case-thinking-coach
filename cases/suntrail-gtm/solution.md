# Facilitator solution

## Reference framework

1. **Customer need and demand:** target segments, occasions, willingness to buy, and cannibalization.
2. **Offer and positioning:** pass rules, customer promise, differentiation, and pilot scope.
3. **Economics:** incremental pass contribution, lost contribution from displaced trips, and fixed launch cost.
4. **GTM execution:** channels, partnerships, timing, and funnel metrics.
5. **Capabilities and risks:** operations, ticketing, misuse, learning plan, and exit criteria.

An alternative may organize around pilot objectives, customer journey, economics, and experiment design.

## Math to disclose after framework submission

- `Pass contribution = Pass price − Variable servicing cost`
- `Cannibalized contribution = Displaced trips × (Trip price − Trip variable cost)`
- `Pilot contribution = Passes × Pass contribution − Cannibalized contribution − Fixed launch cost`

## Worked analysis

Gross pass contribution is `20,000 × ($48 − $9) = $780,000`. Cannibalized trips are `20,000 × 30% × 2 = 12,000`, losing `12,000 × ($18 − $4) = $168,000` of contribution. Pilot contribution is `$780,000 − $168,000 − $500,000 = $112,000`.

## Recommendation

Launch the controlled pilot: it is projected to contribute $112,000 and uses spare capacity. Target occasional leisure travelers, exclude peak commuter periods, and track incremental buyers, cannibalization, activation, and repeat travel. The main risk is higher-than-expected cannibalization; predefine a stop or redesign threshold.
