# SunTrail Weekend Pass Launch

SunTrail is a fictional regional rail operator serving leisure destinations. It is considering a weekend pass offering unlimited Saturday and Sunday travel for one price. Leadership wants to decide whether to launch a six-month pilot and, if so, how to take it to market.

The pilot should generate positive incremental contribution profit while attracting occasional leisure travelers rather than shifting too many existing full-fare trips into the pass.

You may ask 1–2 clarifying questions before presenting your framework. The interviewer may not have every answer.

## Interviewer facts—provide only when requested

- The pilot covers three leisure routes and has sufficient off-peak capacity.
- A pass would cost $48.
- Expected pilot demand is 20,000 passes.
- Variable servicing cost is $9 per pass.
- Launch marketing and setup cost totals $500,000.
- Thirty percent of buyers would otherwise take two $18 trips; variable cost per trip is $4.
