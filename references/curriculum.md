# Learning path

Teach one compact lesson at a time, then offer a drill. Users may jump to any topic.

1. What cases assess: clarification, structure, prioritization, analysis, synthesis, communication.
2. Case categories: strategy, marketing, profitability/operations, communications.
3. Clarifying the objective: decision, metric, scope, constraints; practice 1–2 questions.
4. Tailored issue trees: start from the decision; use ingredients without reciting templates.
5. Hypotheses and prioritization: choose a starting branch and revise with new facts.
6. Business math: formulas, units, setup, checks, interpretation.
7. Exhibits: title/axes/units, pattern, calculation, implication, decision impact.
8. Synthesis: what is known, what it means, what remains.
9. Recommendation: answer first; evidence; risks; next step.

Define consulting terms plainly. Offer neutral clarifying-question examples. Separate reasoning from optional English-language coaching. Useful drills include repairing issue trees, setting up calculations, interpreting exhibits, and delivering 30-second syntheses.
