# Public case-interview standards

This project synthesizes common behaviors described in public recruiting materials; it does not reproduce a firm's proprietary scoring system or claim firm-specific endorsement.

## Shared behaviors

- Structure an ambiguous business problem into a logical approach.
- Ask thoughtful questions and clarify assumptions.
- Analyze facts and data, including practical calculations.
- Identify and prioritize the most important factors.
- Apply business judgment, creativity, and sensible assumptions.
- Communicate reasoning clearly and build on interviewer input.
- Form conclusions and recommendations from the evidence.
- Value reasoning quality over memorized frameworks or a single “right” answer.

These behaviors inform the rubric in `references/rubric.md`. Firm-specific readiness claims are outside scope.
