# Business case formula handbook

State units and time periods. Distinguish revenue, contribution profit, and operating profit.

## Profitability and unit economics

- `Profit = Revenue − Total cost`
- `Revenue = Price × Volume`
- `Total cost = Fixed cost + Variable cost per unit × Volume`
- `Contribution margin per unit = Price − Variable cost per unit`
- `Contribution margin % = Contribution margin per unit ÷ Price`
- `Break-even volume = Fixed cost ÷ Contribution margin per unit`

## Growth and market sizing

- `Growth rate = (New value − Old value) ÷ Old value`
- `CAGR = (Ending value ÷ Beginning value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1`
- `Market size = Customers × Purchases per customer × Average price`
- `Market share = Company sales ÷ Total market sales`

## Pricing

- `Incremental profit = New profit − Baseline profit`
- `Price elasticity = % change in quantity ÷ % change in price`
- Willingness to pay is evidence-based: triangulate research, alternatives, and value created.

## Customer and marketing

- `CAC = Acquisition spend ÷ New customers acquired`
- `Gross-margin LTV = Average revenue per customer × Gross margin % × Average customer lifetime`
- `Funnel outcome = Starting audience × conversion rate at each stage`
- `ROAS = Attributed revenue ÷ Ad spend`
- `Marketing ROI = (Incremental contribution profit − Campaign cost) ÷ Campaign cost`
- `Incremental lift = Observed outcome − Credible counterfactual outcome`

Do not call attributed results incremental without an experiment or defensible counterfactual. Do not equate impressions, clicks, or sentiment with business impact.

## Communications and scenarios

Use `Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Audience response → Organizational outcomes`. State when no essential calculation exists.

- `Expected value = Σ(probability × outcome)`
- `Weighted average = Σ(weight × value) ÷ Σ(weights)`
- Sensitivity analysis varies uncertain inputs and observes decision robustness.
