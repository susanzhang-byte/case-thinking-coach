# Student comfort and case safety

## Purpose and independence

This independent educational project supports students' learning and career preparation through respectful, low-risk business cases. It is not sponsored, endorsed, reviewed, or approved by any university, employer, consulting firm, or other organization. The author's views and work are personal and do not represent their employer. References to public interview-preparation materials do not imply affiliation or endorsement.

The case bank excludes sensitive, distressing, discriminatory, graphic, and personally intrusive scenarios. Generated cases use the same policy. A student may skip, stop, replace, or soften a case immediately without explanation.

## Excluded themes

Reject or safely transform scenarios involving violence, death, weapons, war, terrorism, civil unrest, abuse, harassment, discrimination, hate, sexual content, self-harm, mental-health crises, substance abuse, disease outbreaks, medical emergencies, disasters, crime, active litigation, personal scandals, layoffs, termination, immigration/visa jeopardy, financial hardship, homelessness, food insecurity, minors, vulnerable populations, political/religious conflict, personal identity as a complication, or graphic and humiliating material.

Use identity-neutral names and avoid stereotypes. Do not ask a student to defend unethical conduct or trivialize hardship.

## Approved low-risk crisis patterns

- confusing or poorly received advertising;
- benign product-quality complaints without health or safety effects;
- service outages without safety consequences;
- misinformation about a fictional brand;
- a non-personal executive messaging mistake;
- employee confusion during a non-layoff reorganization;
- customer backlash to a price or policy change;
- partner or influencer message misalignment;
- sustainability-claim credibility questions;
- a leaked, nonconfidential campaign plan.

## Three-gate screen

1. **Theme gate:** choose only eligible roles, industries, and low-risk patterns.
2. **Construction gate:** keep prompt, data, exhibits, and stakeholder tensions within the approved pattern.
3. **Full-case gate:** inspect candidate material, hidden solution, source inspiration, and wording. Reject uncertainty rather than exposing the draft.

For an excluded request, respond briefly: “I can help practice the same business skill using a lower-risk scenario. I'll replace the sensitive context while preserving the learning objective.”

If a student shares personal or sensitive information, minimize repetition, do not score identity or circumstances, offer to stop/change the case, and redirect to the business exercise.

## Bundled-case review

A human reviewer must confirm that each case contains no excluded theme, avoids stereotypes, has an ethical objective, uses neutral language and fictional names, permits immediate exit, and passes calculation and provenance checks.
